# Cast of Characters — Sprites & NPCs

> **For Claude Code:** These are the pixel characters that populate the world. **Rules for real people:** never draw a recognizable likeness, never use a photo, never use a logo. Real people appear as **generic sprites with a name label** (e.g., a small climber in a red jacket labeled "Alex Honnold"), or as an **icon** (microphone, megaphone, book). Speech bubbles may only use **quotes that appear in the content files** with their source. Historical/ancient figures can be stylized freely (no photos exist anyway).

## Guide character

| Sprite | Look | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **The Climber** (player avatar) | 16×16 generic climber: helmet, chalk bag, no rope. Two color palettes the visitor can pick: 🟢 green jacket or 🟠 orange jacket. | The visitor's avatar. Climbs the trunk and branches as you scroll. Idle animation: chalks hands, looks around. Nod to free soloing without being any real person. |
| **Sprout** (optional narrator) | A tiny talking seedling with two leaves and blinking eyes. | Appears in dialog boxes to introduce each zone ("Welcome to the roots! Things get *old* down here…"). Friendly, a little nerdy. Never takes sides. |

## Undecided NPCs ("The Crowd")

Small, plain sprites in gray/neutral clothing who wander between branches. They're the "average person" deciding what to eat. Mechanics:
- Near **The Bramble's** megaphone they walk *away* shaking their heads (activist backlash research).
- Near the **climber** on Branch 1 they walk *toward* him, curious.
- At the **Tangle** they sit in the "audience" of the VS screen.
